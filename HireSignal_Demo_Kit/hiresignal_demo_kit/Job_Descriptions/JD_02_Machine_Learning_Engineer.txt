Machine Learning Engineer - NLP
===============================
Company: SignalForge AI
Location: Remote - India

About the role
--------------
Build and productionize NLP systems for document intelligence and ranking. The role combines model development, evaluation, APIs, and ML infrastructure.

Required qualifications
-----------------------
2+ years of experience in machine learning engineering or applied ML.
Strong Python and SQL.
Hands-on scikit-learn and at least one deep learning framework such as PyTorch.
NLP experience including text classification, embeddings, similarity, or information extraction.
FastAPI or Flask for model-serving APIs.
Docker and basic AWS experience.
Model evaluation using precision, recall, F1, and error analysis.
Experience with MLflow or another experiment/model tracking tool.

Nice to have
------------
Airflow or another workflow orchestrator.
PostgreSQL, Redis, Kubernetes.
Transformers, vector search, or retrieval systems.
Experience with resume, document, or enterprise text analytics.

Responsibilities
----------------
Develop NLP features and ranking/classification models.
Create reproducible training and evaluation pipelines.
Expose models through tested, rate-limited APIs.
Track model experiments and investigate failure modes.
Work with product engineers to ship ML features into customer workflows.

Success in the first 90 days
----------------------------
Ship at least one production-quality feature and document the design.
Improve one measurable quality metric such as latency, reliability, model F1, or user conversion.
Add or strengthen automated tests and observability around owned functionality.
